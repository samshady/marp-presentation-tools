## Layout

- Padding: 50px all sides
- Logo: top-left, 150px wide, 30px offset (baked into theme)
- Cards: white fill, `0.75px solid #6EC8FF` border, 8px border-radius
- Table header: `#B6E3FF` bg, black text
- Table rows: white + `#FAFAFB` alternating
- Bullets: custom `–` dash in `#6EC8FF`
